# Sportmate NoBS 🏋️‍♂️🔒

**The lightweight, privacy-first alternative for Sportmate users.**

Sportmate NoBS is an open-source, reverse-engineered client for the Sportmate platform, essential for accessing gyms across Hungary. Ditch the bloatware and enjoy a faster, safer experience.

---

### 🚀 Why switch?

*   **Privacy First:** Zero telemetry, no tracking, and absolutely no spyware. Your data stays yours.
*   **Ultra Lightweight:** A fraction of the size of the official app. Save storage space and data.
*   **Instant Access:** Log in once via the secure web view, and your entry QR code is always kept with you.
*   **Material You Design:** A modern, beautiful interface that adapts to your phone's theme and colors. 🎨

### 🛠️ How it works
This app provides a clean wrapper for the Sportmate web interface to handle account creation and login. Once authenticated, it securely extracts your personal QR code and stores it locally on your device. This means you can open the app and scan into your gym instantly without running the heavy background processes of the official app.
