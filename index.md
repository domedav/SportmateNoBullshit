## Why choose NoBS over the Official App?

If you are looking for a **Sportmate alternative** that respects your privacy and doesn't drain your battery, you found it.

| Feature | ❌ Official Sportmate App | ✅ Sportmate NoBS (This App) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Privacy** | Tracking & Analytics | **Zero Telemetry / No Tracking** |
| **Size** | Bloated (Large) | **Lightweight (~3MB)** |
| **Speed** | Slow loading times | **Instant Launch** |
| **Code** | Closed Source | **100% Open Source** |
| **Convenience** | Cluttered Interface | **Instant QR Access** |

## Key Features

### 🔒 Privacy Focused
We reversed-engineered the API to provide a clean experience. No ads, no analytics, no "bullshit". Your data stays between you and the gym.

### ⚡ Blazing Fast
Built with modern native Android technology (Jetpack Compose). The app saves your login session, so you don't have to type your password every time. Open the app, and your QR code is ready to refresh instantly.

### 🎨 Material You Design
Looks and feels like a native part of your phone. Adapts to your system colors (Light/Dark mode supported).

## How to Install

1.  **[Download the latest APK](https://github.com/domedav/SportmateNoBullshit/releases/download/1.0/app-release.apk)** file.
2.  Open the file on your Android device.
3.  Allow "Install from Unknown Sources" if prompted (this is necessary for apps outside the Play Store).
4.  Log in once, and enjoy fast gym entry!
